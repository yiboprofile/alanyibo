Dermat AI. No price at all.

An iPhone and Android app for looking after your skin. You scan a photo and it shows what it may be and how sure the model is. There’s also a skin diary, a routine built for your skin and an AI assistant you can ask at any hour. It’s free, with nothing to buy inside.

What does $0 say about a health app from a student team?

Giving Dermat AI away reached about four times as many people a month as SoundSplit, which charges for minutes. People who pay nothing still trust the answer, though, so every answer ends with a note that it is not a diagnosis.

A skin check is a trust good: you can’t tell whether the answer was right until someone qualified tells you, so what a person will pay depends on what they believe about who made it. What does $0 say about a health app from a student team? In the app, a button at the top of the assistant opens the full notice, and every answer has a flag to report it as wrong.

SoundSplit. Sold by the minute.

An iPhone and Android app that splits a song into its stems, so you can take out the vocals for karaoke or mute the piano to practise along. I charge by the minute because every minute processed costs me money. With a subscription, the heaviest users would cost more than they paid.

Crave Deals. Third, then the street.

I co-founded this coupon company, and run it as COO, to help small local restaurants compete with big chains. We only take a commission on what the diners we bring in spend, so a restaurant pays nothing if we bring no one in. Our business plan placed 3rd in the nation, and since then we have pitched more than 40 restaurants, venture firms and investors.
